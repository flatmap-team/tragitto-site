Tragitto

Travel together, effortlessly.

Tragitto is a smart collaborative trip planner. Create trips, invite friends, and build your itinerary together in a shared chat. Your AI concierge helps plan the perfect day based on your preferences.

  • Forward bookings — Send flight, hotel, and restaurant confirmations to your trip email. They appear on your timeline automatically.
  • AI concierge — Ask for suggestions in chat or tap "AI plan" on any day. It knows your budget, dietary needs, and interests.
  • Plan together — Invite friends, share updates in real time, and see everyone's changes instantly.
  • Smart timeline — Times in each destination's timezone. Check-in and check-out labeled automatically. Reorder with a long press.
  • Works offline — Your itinerary is always with you, even without internet. Syncs when you're back online.
