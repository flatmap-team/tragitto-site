How it works

Three steps to the
perfect trip.

No spreadsheets. No group chat chaos. Just a shared timeline that everyone can see.

1

Create & invite

Start a trip, set your dates, and invite your travel companions. Everyone gets a shared timeline instantly.

2

Forward your bookings

Forward flight, hotel, and activity confirmation emails. Our AI reads them and adds everything to your timeline.

3

Let AI plan your day

Features

Everything your
trip needs.

Smart Timeline

Timezone-aware itinerary with check-in/out labels, category icons, and real-time sync across all travelers.

AI Concierge

Chat with your trip assistant. It plans your day, suggests restaurants, and respects your budget and preferences.

Booking Ingestion

Forward confirmation emails and bookings are automatically parsed and added to your shared timeline.

Push Notifications

Get notified when bookings are added, the AI replies, or a travel companion sends a message.

Export as PDF

Print your itinerary or share it with anyone. Branded PDF with all the details, grouped by day.

Works Offline

Your timeline lives on your device. View and edit even without internet. Changes sync when you're back online.
