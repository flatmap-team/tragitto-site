FAQ

Common questions.

Is Tragitto free?

Yes. The core trip-planning experience — timeline, booking ingestion, AI Concierge, and group sharing — is free. We may introduce optional premium features later, but never lock the basics.

Do my travel companions need to install the app?

Owners and editors do — they can add and modify items. Followers (parents, partners) can install too, or simply receive read-only updates. Either way, no separate accounts are required: a Google sign-in works.

How does forwarding bookings work?

Each trip gets a unique forwarding email address. Forward your flight, hotel, or restaurant confirmations there, and our AI extracts the dates, times, locations, and confirmation numbers automatically — adding them to the shared timeline.

What happens if I'm offline?

Your timeline lives on your device. View, edit, and add items even on a plane. Changes sync the moment you're back online — including AI suggestions you queued.

Who can see my data?

Only people you invite. We don't sell or share your trip data. Followers see only your itinerary and (optional) live location — never your booking references, chat, or notes. Privacy policy →

Which devices does Tragitto support?

iPhone (iOS 16+) and Android (Android 8+). Same account, same data, real-time sync between every device.
