How to use Tragitto

Everything you can do — and exactly how to do it.

01

Getting started

Create your first trip

Tap the + button on the Trips screen. Give your trip a name, add your destination, and pick your start and end dates. Tap Create and your shared timeline opens right away.

Sign in

Use your email to get a 6-digit code, or continue with Google or Apple. There's no password to remember, and the same account syncs across every device.

Find your way around

Your timeline is the heart of each trip. The glowing orb opens the AI Concierge, and the ••• menu (top right) holds sharing, auto-import, notifications, export, and more.

02

Build your itinerary

Add a flight, hotel, or activity

Tap + on the timeline, pick a category, then the type, then fill in the details — title, place, date and time. Every time shows in the local timezone of where it happens, so a flight that lands at 3pm local reads as 3pm.

Attach tickets & documents

Open any item and add your boarding passes or confirmations as PDFs — up to five tickets and five documents per item, so everything travels with you.

Edit or remove an item

Tap an item to edit it, then Save. To remove one, swipe it left and tap Delete.

03

Plan together

Editors vs. followers

Editors can add and change items alongside you. Followers — like family back home — see the plan and your updates but can't edit. You choose each person's role when you invite them.

Invite & share a link

Open the members area or the ••• menu and share your trip link. Anyone who taps it joins in their role — no separate account needed, a Google sign-in works.

The follower view

Followers see your itinerary, schedule, and any location you choose to share — never your booking references, chat, or private notes. A small badge reminds them they're viewing, not editing.

Sync & offline

Your trip lives on your device, so you can view and edit even on a plane. Everything syncs the moment you're back online, and you'll see your companions' changes in real time.

04

Let AI do the work

Forward booking emails Premium

Each trip has its own email address. Forward a flight, hotel, or restaurant confirmation and the AI reads it and adds the details to your timeline. Find the address under ••• → Auto-import.

Ask the AI Concierge Premium

Tap the orb to chat. Ask for restaurant ideas, a walking route, or a full day plan — it knows your dates, pace, and budget.

Auto-plan a day Premium

From an empty day or a day header, ask the Concierge to plan it. It suggests a relaxed, realistic day you can accept, tweak, or ignore.

Fix readiness issues Premium

Tragitto flags gaps like a missing hotel or a tight connection, with a readiness score. Tap Fix and the Concierge proposes a solution you can add in a tap.

05

Travel safely & stay informed

Share live location Premium

Open ••• → Share location and pick how long. Your companions can follow your dot for peace of mind, and sharing stops automatically when the time's up.

Check-ins & arrivals

Followers can ask if you're safe, and you can confirm arrivals as you reach each place — keeping everyone reassured without a flood of messages.

Notifications & quiet hours

Open ••• → Notifications to choose what you hear about — reminders, arrivals, suggestions — and set quiet hours so nothing buzzes overnight.

Export your trip as PDF

Open ••• → Export as PDF for a clean, shareable itinerary with every day and detail — handy for offline backups or sharing outside the app.

Emergency contact card

Keep emergency details on hand and share them in a tap, so help is never more than a moment away.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is Tragitto free?

Yes — planning is free. Download the app, create trips, build your timeline by hand, invite companions, and share in real time at no cost. The AI Concierge and email & booking forwarding are Premium, unlocked per trip.

What does Premium cost?

Premium is a one-time purchase per trip during our beta — you'll see the exact price in the app before you buy. It unlocks the AI Concierge and booking forwarding for that trip, with a generous AI allowance. Need more? You can top up tokens anytime. Premium is available on both iPhone and Android.

When does Premium activate and expire?

Premium activates the moment you create the trip and stays active until shortly after the trip ends — so it's there throughout your planning and travel. Each new trip is its own purchase.

Do my travel companions need to install the app?

Owners and editors do — they can add and modify items. Followers (parents, partners) can install too, or simply receive read-only updates. Either way, no separate accounts are required: a Google sign-in works.

What's the difference between an editor and a follower?

Editors add and change items with you. Followers see the plan and your updates — including any location you share — but can't edit. Pick a role when you invite someone.

How does forwarding bookings work?

Each trip gets a unique forwarding email address. Forward your flight, hotel, or restaurant confirmations there, and our AI extracts the dates, times, locations, and confirmation numbers automatically — adding them to the shared timeline.

Do my bookings get added automatically?

Yes, on Premium. Forward a confirmation to your trip's email address and the AI extracts the dates, times, places, and confirmation numbers and adds them to your timeline.

What happens if I'm offline?

Your timeline lives on your device. View, edit, and add items even on a plane. Changes sync the moment you're back online — including AI suggestions you queued.

Can I use the app offline, like on a plane?

Absolutely. Your trip lives on your device — view, add, and edit anytime. Everything syncs when you're back online, including AI suggestions you queued.

Who can see my data?

Only people you invite. We don't sell or share your trip data. Followers see only your itinerary and (optional) live location — never your booking references, chat, or notes.

Is my location private?

Yes. Location sharing is off until you start it, you choose how long, and it stops automatically. Only people on your trip can see it, and followers never see your bookings, chat, or notes.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Premium is a one-time per-trip purchase, so there's nothing to cancel. For refunds, use the standard process on the App Store or Google Play.

Which devices does Tragitto support?

iPhone (iOS 16+) and Android (Android 8+). Same account, same data, real-time sync between every device.

What languages does Tragitto support?

The app is available in English, Portuguese, and Spanish, and we're adding more.
